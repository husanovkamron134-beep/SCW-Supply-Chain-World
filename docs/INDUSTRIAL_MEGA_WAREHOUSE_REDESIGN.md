# SCW Industrial Mega Warehouse — process-flow redesign

This is a separate preview page and does not replace `mega-warehouse-5xl-ops.html`.

## Layout
- 160 × 140 m building footprint; 12 non-overlapping process zones.
- Inbound, Quality Control and Returns on the north receiving side.
- Reserve Storage, High-Bay AS/RS and Replenishment in the central storage block.
- Picking, Sorting and Packing form the downstream fulfilment block.
- Outbound Staging and Dispatch sit on the south shipping side.
- Control Center is elevated as a mezzanine.

## Equipment and visual flow
- Multi-row pallet rack blocks with beams and stored pallet loads.
- High-bay stacker crane and lift carriage.
- Branching conveyor lines between sorting and packing.
- Dock doors and trailers, QC benches, returns lanes, packing cells.
- Forklift and AGV visual motion loops.

## Limitations
- Vehicle motion is illustrative and not yet collision-aware.
- Conveyor parcels and stock are not yet driven by live WMS tasks.
- This is a new visual prototype; preserve the previous operational page.
- Validate on real mobile and desktop browsers before replacing the primary preview.
